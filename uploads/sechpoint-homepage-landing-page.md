# SechPoint Homepage Landing Page Design

## Page Purpose

Create a clean enterprise-style homepage for **SechPoint** that communicates a unified network intelligence platform for telecoms, ISPs, and sovereign cyber environments.

The page should follow a strong B2B enterprise sequence:

1. Hero
2. Trust / partner logo strip
3. Broad platform value section
4. Feature block 1
5. Feature block 2
6. Feature block 3
7. Testimonial / proof statement
8. Platform or solution card section
9. Security / compliance section
10. Final CTA banner

This flow works well for SechPoint because the platform story covers AIG appliances, DPI intelligence, analytics, cyber threat detection, subscriber analytics, compliance filtering, and support-led deployment.

## Design Notes

- Use a polished enterprise SaaS visual style.
- Keep the hero clear, confident, and security-focused.
- Use alternating feature sections to create rhythm and scannability.
- Use dark cards for proof, platform, and final CTA moments.
- Avoid overloading the page with too many technical terms at once.
- Use icons or simple diagrams for DPI, cyber intelligence, subscriber analytics, and compliance control.
- Maintain strong contrast and clear CTA hierarchy throughout the page.
